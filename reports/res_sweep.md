## Adoption

- `max_pixels` 2196480 (2145 visual tokens) is adopted. This is the native page, 1240 x 1754 px, so no
  upscaling is involved.
- `max_pixels` 1843200 (1750 tokens) is rejected: its OVERALL CI lower bound is -2.33 pts, not > 0.
- Spec section 11 item 2 is the pre-registered rule; fine-tuned retraining at the new resolution
  still requires fold 0 to pass G4.

## Caveats

- The CI is on 40 documents and is wide. OVERALL is the only decisive criterion; every other delta is
  reported, not decisive.
- The adopted resolution's digital-slice OVERALL delta is +4.76 with CI [-0.89, +10.58], which
  includes 0; the gain is established on the scanned slice and on all documents, not on digital alone.
- The control was rerun at batch size 1. The production batch size is 8, so peak VRAM at batch 8 is
  UNMEASURED for the adopted resolution. The 02 bench measured a batch-8 peak of 13.6 GiB at 1260
  tokens (figure quoted from that bench, not recomputed here); at 2145 tokens batch 8 may not fit in
  14.5 GiB. A native-resolution bench at batch 8 must decide the production batch size.
- Cost: s/page rises 36.6 -> 38.3
  (+4.7%) at batch 1.
